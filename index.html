Wall Memory Law

An elliptical cavity whose wall patches can be written (a Robin spring, after Sol's Kavitaatio). A ping from one focus reads the wall at the other. Several written patches interact: the echo is not the sum of their single echoes. A k-patch interaction can only arrive along a path that touches all k patches, so it switches on at that path's travel time. The listening window therefore sets the polynomial degree of the memory, and the geometry predicts it. Click wall patches to write them, ping, then measure their interaction.
